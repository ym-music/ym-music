ENTRANCE EXAM

Contemporary Music Entrance-Exam Academy in Dongtan · YM MUSICEntrance exams aren't about instinct —
they're about preparation.

"I get the artistry, but what exactly does my child need to prepare?" — this is what parents of exam students most want to know. For vocal, guitar, jazz piano, composition, MIDI and drums, the standards for practical, theory and ear training differ by major, and so does the kind of portfolio each university wants. YM MUSIC prepares for those differences precisely, major by major.

Practical· Theory· Ear training· Curriculum by major· Portfolio· Mock exams· Admissions counseling· Practical· Theory· Ear training· Curriculum by major· Portfolio· Mock exams· Admissions counseling·

University entrance prep has a different exam structure for every major. A vocal practical and a composition practical are completely different events, and even the ear-training test alone demands a different sense in each major.

That's why YM MUSIC runs six majors — vocal · guitar · jazz piano · composition · MIDI · drums — as separate, independent curricula.

Curriculum by major

Even within the same "entrance exam," the standards for practical, theory and ear training differ by major. Check each of the six majors.

01 · VOCAL

Vocal

PracticalRequired and free-choice pieces: tone, expression and stage manner; song selection matched to each university's practical style
TheoryPreparing for each university's past contemporary-music theory question types; basic harmony
Ear trainingPitch, rhythm and melody dictation; repeated drills on each university's ear-test formats
CurriculumRepeated mock practicals raise your on-stage, real-exam readiness
02 · GUITAR

Guitar

PracticalRequired pieces separated for electric and bass guitar; balance of rhythm and solo parts
TheoryChord theory, scales
Ear trainingRhythm dictation, listening to chord progressions
CurriculumBuild improvisational readiness and your own sound together
03 · JAZZ PIANO

Jazz Piano

PracticalPlaying jazz standards; improvised soloing and comping
TheoryJazz harmony
Ear trainingChord and melody dictation
CurriculumTrain the balance between accompaniment (comping) and soloing together
04 · COMPOSITION

Composition

PracticalPresenting original compositions; arranging practical
TheoryHarmony and counterpoint basics
Ear trainingHarmonic dictation, melody dictation
CurriculumCoaching centered on the finish of your portfolio pieces
05 · MIDI

MIDI

PracticalDAW practice, track production
TheoryBasic theory of sound engineering and mixing
Ear trainingSound-analysis listening
CurriculumMake portfolio beats and tracks from scratch to completion
06 · DRUM

Drums

PracticalPlaying required pieces; rhythmic accuracy and dynamic control
TheoryRhythm theory; understanding meter structures
Ear trainingRhythm dictation; accuracy in hearing groove
CurriculumTrain a steady groove and band-ensemble readiness together
